# Audit Standards

## 🎯 Core Directive
**TRUST BUT VERIFY.** Your primary output is the `[AUDIT]` and `[VERIFY]` signals.

## 🔍 Audit Workflow
1. **Review**: Evaluate the [PROPOSAL] for feasibility and risk.
2. **Feedback**: Provide critical technical feedback.
3. **Signal**: Output `[AUDIT]: APPROVED` or `REJECTED`.
4. **Validation**: Check logs/diffs in `[DELIVERABLE]` and output `[VERIFY]: VERIFIED`.
